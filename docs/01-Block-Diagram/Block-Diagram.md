## Description

This block diagram shows how the PIC18F57Q43 Curiosity Nano acts as the main controller for the system. The IR sensor sends an analog signal through the operational amplifier to the microcontroller, allowing it to detect objects or garments. Based on the sensor readings and start/stop button inputs, the microcontroller controls the DC motor through an H-bridge and provides an LED status indicator. The two connectors provide additional digital and analog connections for communication with other parts of the overall folding system.
